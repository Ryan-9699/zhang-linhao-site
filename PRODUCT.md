# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Recruiters, creative leads, live-production teams, AI video teams, and brand-content collaborators who need to quickly judge Zhang Linhao's visual judgment, production credibility, AIGC fluency, and ability to handle high-pressure projects.

## Product Purpose

This site is a personal portfolio and resume surface for Zhang Linhao. It should make his background in live production, cinematography, AI visual generation, and large-scale project execution feel immediate, premium, and technically credible. Success means a visitor understands his strengths within one minute, remembers the visual personality, and can inspect key projects in depth.

## Brand Personality

Kinetic, cinematic, exacting.

The brand should feel like a live-production control room crossed with an Awwwards motion portfolio: precise, image-led, restless, and confident. The visual language should be experimental enough to signal taste, but controlled enough to support a professional hiring decision.

## Anti-references

Avoid generic AI-purple SaaS gradients, centered template hero sections, glassy feature-card grids, oversized resume text, empty decorative blobs, and static PDF-resume energy. Avoid designs where every section looks like the same card repeated with different copy.

## Design Principles

- Show production credibility through real project imagery, numbers, and detailed case narratives.
- Treat motion as proof of visual taste, not decoration.
- Keep the page spacious and readable despite the experimental interaction layer.
- Use image-first storytelling for key projects, with text arranged as editorial evidence.
- Make contact and professional details easy to find after the visual impression lands.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target readable contrast, keyboard-accessible project detail dialogs, meaningful image alt text, and reduced-motion fallbacks. Motion may be expressive on desktop, but content must remain available without animation.
